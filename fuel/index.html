Methodology & limits of this tool

This is a client-side calculator implementing the scoring rules, decision thresholds, override rules, and conflict-resolution hierarchy from the source spec exactly as written, for both regular gasoline and on-road diesel scored independently. It cannot auto-fetch RBOB/ULSD futures, GasBuddy, or AAA data: those either require paid/authenticated feeds or block browser-based (CORS) requests from a static site with no backend. You look the numbers up (links above) and enter them; the engine does the arithmetic, applies the override and price-jump-warning rules, generates the report in the required format, and lets you log outcomes over time for calibration. Never invent a value — leave a field blank and the confidence rating will reflect what's missing, exactly as the spec requires.

Do not give scoring weight to the S&P 500, Dow, Nasdaq, generic inflation stories, political commentary, unsourced social-media rumors, national averages alone, a single station, or crude oil alone — this tool doesn't collect any of those as inputs on purpose.
